# Aircraft inspection assets

- Fixed-wing survey model, adapted from [Fixed Wing Surveillance Drone](https://3dassets.dev/assets/aircraft-fleet-and-airfield-surveillance-drone-79504ea8) by 3D Assets. CC0 1.0.
- Multirotor model, adapted from [Heavy Lift Quadcopter Drone](https://3dassets.dev/assets/aircraft-fleet-and-airfield-quadcopter-drone-bf85eb66) by 3D Assets. CC0 1.0.
- Apron texture from [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02) by Rob Tuytel and Poly Haven. CC0 1.0.

The two aircraft assets are listed by their publisher as AI-assisted. Vektr changes their materials, scene framing and overlays. Models are visual stand-ins for the fictional fleet. The cutaway shows an illustrative battery module.
